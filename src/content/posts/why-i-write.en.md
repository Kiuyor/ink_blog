## Preface

I started this blog to keep a corner that is **completely my own**.

WeChat Official Accounts, Juejin, CSDN — they're all great, but they're ultimately "someone else's land": algorithms decide who sees you, the rules change overnight, and the articles are essentially platform assets. A self-hosted static blog, by contrast, has a permanent URL, no ads, no moderation, no subscription lock-in — it's more like your **professional business card**.

## What I'll Write

- Tinkering notes on tech (pitfalls, retrospectives, principles)
- Fragmented thoughts from books / papers
- Some half-baked ideas I still want to leave a trace of

## How to Use This Blog

Browse freely, comment freely. If some post helped you, that is its greatest meaning.

---

If you've been thinking about writing something too, don't wait until you're "ready" — writing it down matters more than writing it perfectly.
