Hi, I'm **ink**.

This is my personal blog *ink_blog*, subtitled "磨叽的墨迹" (roughly: the ink stains of a dilly-dallying writer) — most of the time I do dawdle, but I always leave a little ink behind.

::tip{title="About this blog"}
It's a fully static site built with **Astro + Svelte** — no backend, no database, just Markdown. Speed, privacy, and permanence are what I care about most.
::

## What I tinker with

- Front-end / full-stack development
- A bit of design and a typography obsession
- The itch to explain complex things simply

## A few things this blog cares about

- **Privacy first**: self-hosted Umami for analytics, no third-party cookies, the data is mine.
- **Leave a comment**: every post has a Giscus comment box — just sign in with your GitHub account.
- **Themeable**: the palette button in the top-right lets you switch the accent color to taste.
- **Machine-friendly**: every page ships structured data plus an `llms.txt`, so it's easy to index and cite.

## Where to find me

The GitHub link in the top-right will reach me ([Kiuyor](https://github.com/Kiuyor)). Comments under any post are welcome too.
